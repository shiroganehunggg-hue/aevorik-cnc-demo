01 / MATERIAL REMOVAL
⌘

CNC Milling

CNC milling uses rotating cutting tools to remove material from a workpiece, creating features such as flat faces, pockets, slots and contoured surfaces.

Typical examples: Brackets, housings, fixtures, and flat or contoured components.

02 / ROTATIONAL MACHINING
◉

CNC Turning

CNC turning rotates the workpiece while a cutting tool forms cylindrical features such as shafts, bushings, bores and threads.

Typical examples: Shafts, bushings, threaded adapters, and cylindrical components.
